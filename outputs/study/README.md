# Extended study — results behind the technical report

Everything in this directory is generated by the scripts in [`../../scripts`](../../scripts) and
is committed so the report's numbers can be checked without re-running anything. The main
pipeline's own outputs (10 behavioral samples + 3 edits) stay one level up in
[`../`](..); this directory holds the six experiments added for the final milestone.

Model: `pythia-160m` unless a table says otherwise. Device: CPU. Seed: 0. Decoding: greedy.

## Experiment 5 — behavior across models and scales

All 48 prompts, three models (mean logit difference; accuracy in parentheses).

| Model | Agreement | Factual recall | Induction | All |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpt2 (124M) | +4.50 (1.00) | +2.79 (0.92) | +2.45 (0.94) | +3.39 (0.96) |
| pythia-160m | +4.62 (1.00) | +2.07 (0.83) | +1.73 (0.81) | +3.02 (0.90) |
| pythia-410m | +5.12 (1.00) | **+4.41 (1.00)** | +2.42 (0.88) | +4.04 (0.96) |

Agreement is saturated everywhere. Factual recall is the behavior that scales: +2.07 → +4.41 and
0.83 → 1.00 accuracy from 160m to 410m. GPT-2 beats pythia-160m overall despite being smaller,
so parameter count alone does not order these models.

## Experiment 6 — the edit layer matters less than the tracing rule suggests

Editing each target at *every* layer (36 fits) shows the fact can be rewritten from most early
layers, and never from the last three:

| Target | Layers with efficacy 1.0 | Layers tried |
|---|---|---|
| Mount Everest | 0–8 | 12 |
| The Eiffel Tower | 0–5, 7, 8 | 12 |
| The Mona Lisa | 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 | 12 |

Scoring each layer-selection rule by the swept quality of the layer it picks (5 tracing seeds ×
3 targets):

| Rule | Mean efficacy | Mean generalization | Mean top-1 preserved | Distinct layers picked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw argmax (`window=1`, the default) | **0.933** | 0.033 | 0.800 | 7 |
| windowed argmax (`window=3`) | 0.733 | 0.167 | 0.500 | 4 |
| windowed argmax (`window=5`) | 0.800 | 0.167 | 0.667 | 5 |

The multi-layer window — the fix this project's earlier README proposed for the unreliable
tracing peak — makes layer selection **worse**, so `edit_layer_window` stays at 1. The
implementation is kept (`models/interpret.py::best_edit_layer`) because it is the ROME-style
rule; the measurement is the reason it is not the default.

## Experiment 7 — one behavior is carried by one head, two are diffuse

Top head per behavior, and how much that same head matters to the other behaviors:

| Behavior | Top head | Drop on own behavior | Mean drop on others | Selectivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agreement | **L6.H4** | +2.29 | −0.02 | **1.00** |
| Induction | L0.H0 | +1.19 | +0.17 | 0.75 |
| Factual recall | L0.H7 | +1.89 | +0.60 | 0.52 |

L6.H4 is a genuinely dedicated agreement head: ablating it costs 2.29 logits of agreement margin
and *nothing* elsewhere. Factual recall's top head is half-generic — it is also worth +0.94 to
agreement — which is what a diffuse mechanism looks like under this measurement.

Across all three behaviors the single largest component is **MLP 0** (drops of +4.96 agreement,
+3.59 induction, +2.69 factual recall), i.e. shared input processing rather than behavior-specific
circuitry. See `ablation_top_components.csv` for the full ranking and `ablation_mlp.png` for the
per-layer MLP profile.

## Experiment 8 — the three behaviors emerge at different times

Mean logit difference at 11 Pythia checkpoints (step 0 = random initialization):

| Step | 0 | 128 | 512 | 1 000 | 2 000 | 4 000 | 8 000 | 16 000 | 64 000 | 143 000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agreement | +0.02 | +0.01 | +0.15 | **+2.03** | +3.53 | +4.28 | +4.30 | +4.60 | +5.11 | +4.62 |
| Factual recall | +0.02 | −0.40 | −0.70 | +0.08 | +0.42 | +0.86 | +1.86 | +2.44 | **+2.90** | +2.07 |
| Induction | −0.02 | +0.02 | +0.08 | +0.25 | +0.84 | +1.13 | +1.68 | +1.41 | **+1.96** | +1.73 |

Three distinct shapes: agreement switches on between steps 512 and 2 000 and is essentially done
by 4 000; induction rises steadily from step 1 000; factual recall is *actively wrong* around
steps 128–512 (the model prefers the incorrect capital) before recovering and climbing slowly.
For two of the three behaviors the **final checkpoint is not the best one** — 64 000 scores higher
on both factual recall and induction than 143 000.

## Experiment 9 — the same edit, three models

Mean over the 3 targets, default hyperparameters (`edit_kl_weight = 0.0625`):

| Model | Efficacy | Generalization | Specificity | Top-1 preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpt2 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.33 | 1.00 |
| pythia-160m | 0.67 | 0.00 | 0.33 | 0.83 |
| pythia-410m | **1.00** | **0.50** | 0.50 | 1.00 |

Read alone, this says the method works better at scale and not at all on GPT-2. Experiment 10
shows that reading is wrong.

## Experiment 10 — why those hyperparameters do not transfer

The edit objective penalizes the injected vector in absolute terms
($-\log p(o^*) + \lambda\lVert\delta\rVert^2$), but the size of a vector that changes the
prediction scales with the residual stream it is added to. At the edited layer the mean residual
norm is **75.0 for gpt2** against 13.4 (pythia-160m) and 18.9 (pythia-410m) — so the same
$\lambda$ is a far harsher constraint for GPT-2, and the optimizer drives $\delta$ to 2.76 instead
of the ~100 that model needs. That is the entire GPT-2 "failure".

Pooled over 3 models × 3 targets:

| `edit_kl_weight` | Mean ‖δ‖ | Efficacy | Generalization | Top-1 preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0625 (default) | 5.1 | 0.556 | 0.167 | **0.944** |
| 0.01 | 12.4 | **0.889** | 0.444 | 0.722 |
| 0.001 | 29.6 | **0.889** | 0.500 | 0.389 |
| 0 | 91.3 | 0.778 | **0.611** | 0.000 |

Generalization is therefore **not** structurally zero, as the earlier single-configuration run
implied — it was suppressed by the penalty. At `edit_kl_weight = 0.01` GPT-2 goes from efficacy
0.00 to 0.67 and pythia-160m reaches 1.00, and pythia-410m reaches efficacy 1.00 with
generalization 0.83 and top-1 preserved 0.83. Removing the penalty entirely maximizes
generalization and destroys specificity: every neighbourhood prediction changes.

**The default was not changed.** `edit_kl_weight` stays at 0.0625 so the committed pipeline output
remains the conservative, high-specificity configuration the earlier milestones reported, and the
trade-off is documented here instead of being silently re-tuned.
